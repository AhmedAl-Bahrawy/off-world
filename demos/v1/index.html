01 — THE COLLAPSEEARTH / 2231

A CIVILIZATION BUILT ON BORROWED KNOWLEDGE

We forgot
how to make.

Humanity did not run out of ideas. It ran out of people who knew how to turn them into things.

For generations, the systems that made life possible were owned by a handful of institutions. Manufacturing, medicine, energy, food, infrastructure — every essential process became a sealed service.

People learned how to request. They forgot how to repair. And when the systems began to fail, there was no one left who understood how to rebuild them.
